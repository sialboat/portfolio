List of Relevant Coursework that I have taken / plan to take during my academic career at NYU. Any planned courses are *italicized*.

Music Technology courses are from the NYU Steinhardt Music Technology program, which runs through the Department of Music and Performing Arts Professions (MPAP)
Music Theory courses are from the NYU Steinhardt Music Theory and Composition program, which runs through the Department of Music and Performing Arts Professions (MPAP)

Computer Science courses are from the Courant School of Computing, Mathematics, and Data Science.
	
Electrical Engineering courses are from the Tandon School of Engineering department of Electrical Engineering.

## Music Technology

### MPATE-UE 1817 & 1828 Analog Electronics
- Learned the fundamentals of electricity with an emphasis on creating musical devices (ranges from effects to oscillators) on a breadboard. Culminated in a final project where I created a vactrol-based tremolo with a slew-limiter that can adjust the tremolo shape.
### MPATE-UE 1818 & 1828 Digital Electronics
- Learned the fundamentals of C++ and Embedded Audio Programming using a Teensy and the Arduino IDE with emphasis on creating devices ranging from MIDI sequencers to synthesizer effects. Culminated in a final project where I created a rudimentary game engine where the positional and directional information of the game engine's character can be used to control VST plugins.
### MPATE-UE 1001 Analog Recording Technology
- Learned about the different kinds of microphones, various stereo recording techniques (AB, NOS, ORTS), and its implications within an analog console. Recorded a song with peers to tape using an AMPEX 16-track Tape Machine.
### MPATE-UE 1003 Digital Recording Technology
- Learned the fundamentals of digital signal processing with an emphasis on analog to digital conversion / audio digitization. Utilizing Pro Tools to record and create music within different workflows with peers (hybrid, fully in the box, etc.). Culminated in a final project collaboration with a vocal performance major
### MPATE-UE Audio For Video I (Sep 2024 - Dec 2024)
- Learned the fundamental elements and components that make up a typical audio-post workflow, from ADR and foley recording to spotting, sound design, and collaboration. Culminated in a final group project re-designing all the sound in an excerpt from *Arcane* (Riot Games, Netflix).
### MPATE-UE Audio for Video II (Jan 2025 - May 2025)
- Learned select advanced topics within a typical audio-post environment, ranging from editing stock music and sonic branding to mixing and level-riding. Culminated in a final group project re-designing all the sound in an excerpt from *Ready Player One*

### MPATE-GE 2047 Advanced Computer Music (Sep 2025 - Dec 2025)
- Learned about the historical developments in sound synthesis, algorithmic music composition, and sound creation hardware and software. Additionally learned how to implement the techniques described in class using a MOZZI-based ESP32 embedded system. Culminated in a final performance featuring an EEG brain-wave MIDI Controller
