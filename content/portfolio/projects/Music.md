An archive of some of my favorite pieces (finished or unfinished), archived to the [whyp.it](https://whyp.it/users/1444/sialboat) website. My whyp account ranges from work in progress bounces to finished projects.

The long-term plan is to put music exclusively on [bandcamp](https://sialboat.bandcamp.com/), as I despise current streaming platforms and the middleware that is required to place them on said platforms.

[Assignment 7a - Oats](https://whyp.it/tracks/321697/wang-silas-cr7a?token=xkDMs) - Counterpoint study using multiple isorhythms and an extracted dialog from [this youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O7FIiYsVy3U)

[Assignment 7 - the bright places](https://whyp.it/tracks/271461/cr7-bright-places) - Counterpoint study using multiple isorhythms and extracted dialog from the film "All the Bright Places"

[Assignment 4](https://whyp.it/tracks/258674/assignment-4-2025-02-18-0036) - Counterpoint study featuring a cantus firmus that dynamically changes throughout the song's duration.

[Assignment 2a](https://whyp.it/tracks/255986/arjomand-assignment-2a-2025-02-10-0013) - Counterpoint study featuring a cantus firmus and a random drum generator.

[The Last Letter](https://whyp.it/tracks/128416/the-last-letter) - inspired by the dialog from the end of the "Perks of a Wallflower" movie. This one part of the movie really resonated with me, and so I decided to write music to dialog (as if it was a movie), and I think it turned out really well.

[Home](https://whyp.it/tracks/56801/home-nov-30-2022?token=gnO48) - A chill, laid-back hip hop track that features a plethora of sonic flavors that resolve in a triumphant letter documenting where I believe “home” is.[Petrichor](https://whyp.it/tracks/88740/petrichor-apr-13-23) - an unfinished musical adventure that tries to paint the smell of Petrichor, and then expand it through a flurry of Logic MIDI instruments.  

["untitled medieval waltz"](https://whyp.it/tracks/25080/medieval-kinda-waltz-e3OoR) - An orchestral composition / Logic mockup of a “medieval” sounding melody that floated around in my head.  

["bad breakcore"](https://whyp.it/tracks/202343/bad-breakcore-2024-08-30-1706) - a piece featuring very ethereal and retro atmospheres with a driving breakcore breakbeat. It has a pretty cool vibe to it and it's loosely based off of [this one TikTok](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DSH_sYTuRyg)  

[Seven Eight Four](https://whyp.it/tracks/23235/seven-eight-four-jul-6-21-b2O4v) - An unconventional conventional piece of music that switches between 7/4 and 4/4, and follows a “go with the flow” song structure (there isn’t much of a song structure).

[You can't hurry love remix](https://whyp.it/tracks/251756/you-cant-hurry-love-rmx) - a bass music remix of ["You can't hurry love" by The Supremes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ovoBi3pXD_A&pp=ygUieW91IGNhbid0IGh1cnJ5IGxvdmUgdGhlIHN1cHJlbWVzdw%3D%3D), aiming at harmonically transforming the song's tonally stable identity into something far more unstable and unsettling. More information about the project can be found [here](https://sites.google.com/d/1JETKElmP_Dpq_Zw-FHmqOtpgWKYDwF8p/p/1ZFr3fZJY8bzHCeBKWUb8ydCviX5C6viu/edit?pli=1).  

[Smash Mouth All Star](https://youtu.be/_OLXbb_CicU) - An intentionally unfaithful recreation of Smash Mouth's All Star where every sound within the instrumental was made with an excerpt of my biology teacher's voice.  

[No Games Remix](https://whyp.it/tracks/31521/no-games-sialboat-remix-aK7mG) - A song I had stems for a remix contest that I never ended up submitting anything to. For fun, I decided to recreate a "Dua Lipa Levitating" vibe.  

["karma but better"](https://whyp.it/tracks/171616/karma-but-better-apr-10) - hyperpop rendition of Jojo Siwa's "Karma" that turned into a dubstep-jersey club hybrid.

[Psychadelia](https://whyp.it/tracks/251755/luka-psychadelia?token=8l48H) - A piece I did for my roomate's film documentary final project. This piece explores the different stages of a high and is accompanied by a video.  

["build 2"](https://whyp.it/tracks/202348/built-two) - My second piece I ever wrote in Ableton and at NYU. Super simple piano riff that is accompanied by overstimulating IDM-esque percussion.

[A Moment of Magic](https://whyp.it/tracks/127953/a-moment-of-magic-oct-3) - The very first piece I ever wrote in Ableton and at NYU. I sampled the piece ["Momento Magico"](https://open.spotify.com/track/12VnVmBD5uMezscppLrQ25?si=15508edc322949c7) by Youn Sun Nah & Ulf Wakenius and created a goofy little drum and bass track.
