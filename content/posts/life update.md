made it back alive from NYU Paris, my study abroad trip was very fun and insightful. the main questions i'm anticipating:
- my favorite thing I ate abroad was the Shoyu ramen from Kodawari Ramen. a close second place is Berlin Döner. the french pastries were good but i'm not really into that kind of food in general.
- my favorite thing about Paris is the parks and the public transit (this can be said about europe at large)
- my favorite thing I did abroad was seeing Germany (Superbooth, Berlin, Dresden)
- my favorite places i visited in no particular order
	- berlin; superbooth, the autobahn, berlin wall art gallery. i also found it really funny how checkpoint charlie is located right next to a mcdonalds and a kfc
	- dresden / bad schandau; saxon-switzerland national park. hiked a small bit of it and then napped for the rest of the day because i had an ear-nose-throat infection
	- prague; saw my friend keith at prague and had a banger malatang bowl. also their spiked ginger ale whiskey goes hard
	- copenhagen; went to the first adc copenhagen event so i didn't get to see a lot of the city but overall it was very pretty.
	- athens; hades 2 irl!?? hiked up a random hill and dropped my phone, only regret is that the weather was cloudy and we didn't get the chance to see the temple of hephaestus
	- florence; the gelato and sandwiches and the city vibe was night and day compared to that i was used to in paris. definitely the best pizza i have ever had came from spots in florence

what i'm currently up to:
- implementing an embedded version of tralie's concatenator project to the linux beagleboard system. i made it through 3-4 steps of the python homework assignment before giving up from my ineptitude (and distaste) for the python language and began plotting a project out in C++. given that the target is a BELA Gem (see above, beagleboard 2), we have some knobs we can play with that aren't typically available. most notably, we can parallelize the particle filter through OMP
