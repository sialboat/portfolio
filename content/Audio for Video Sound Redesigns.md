## Arcane Sound Re-Design
Group project redesign of a clip from Season 1 Episode 1 of _Arcane_, a show by Riot Games and Fortische.

The aim for this redesign was to sell the show's futuristic steampunk atmosphere and figure out if we could build this style of sound from the ground up ourselves. 

My primary responsibilities for this project included designing the Hard-FX for the blue hextech crystal, recording miscellaneous foley and footsteps, as well as voicing the character "Claggor" and various enforcers.

## Ready Player One Re-Design
Group project redesign of an excerpt from *Ready Player One*, a film directed by Steven Spielberg based on the book by Ernest Cline.

The aim for this redesign was to sell the intensity of the clip as well as the numerous context switches that was constantly being juxtaposed.

My primary responsibilities for this project included designing and implementing all backgrounds for the Oasis and a little bit of the real-world backgrounds, as well as designing the Hard FX in the Oasis.
